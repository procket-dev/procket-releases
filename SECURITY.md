# Security

To report a vulnerability in Procket, email
[security@procket.dev](mailto:security@procket.dev). Please do not open a
public issue.

We will acknowledge your report, keep you updated, and not take action against
good-faith research that avoids other customers' data. Our full policy is at
[procket.dev/security](https://procket.dev/security).

Fixes ship as new releases. Run `procket update`, or update through your package
manager, to get them.
